FAQ

Before you hit send

What happens after I send this?

A human reads it, checks the calendar and replies within one business day with availability and a quote based on your actual event — not a starting-at price.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A signed contract and deposit hold your date; until both are in, the date stays open to other inquiries.

What if we need to change the date?

Tell us as early as you can. If we are free on the new date, we move your booking. Our full policy is spelled out in the contract before you sign anything.

Do you charge travel to Tahoe, Reno, Monterey or wine country?

A flat travel fee, quoted up front with your package. Nothing gets added later.

Not ready to book, just curious?

Send the form anyway with whatever you know so far. We are happy to answer questions with no obligation.
