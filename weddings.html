The night, in three acts

Every part sounds different. On purpose.

Ceremony

Prelude as guests are seated, processional cues you choose, clear wireless mics for the officiant and for the vows so the back row hears every word. Wind on the coast, echo in a chapel, a garden with no power ... we plan for the venue you actually picked.

Cocktail hour

Our favorite hour. Warm, unhurried, a little soulful, and pitched so people can talk without leaning in. Separate sound system on the patio while the room flips, so there is never a silent gap.

Reception

Grand entrance, first dance, toasts, dinner, then a floor that fills on the first song and stays full. We MC with warmth and restraint (announcements, not a comedy set) and we close the night on the song you have been picturing for a year.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Do you MC as well as DJ?

Yes, and it is included. We keep announcements warm, short and clear, your names pronounced correctly, your family honored properly, and no bit that makes anyone wince.

Can you cover a ceremony in a different spot from the reception?

That is The Celebration package setup. We bring separate systems for the ceremony and cocktail area so there is no scramble between locations.

What if our must-play list is very specific and very long?

Bring it. We will play what you love, tell you honestly which songs tend to clear a floor, and always follow your call in the end.

Do you travel to Napa, Sonoma, Monterey and Tahoe weddings?

Every season. Travel is quoted as a flat fee up front, and for mountain and coastal venues we arrive with backup power and weather contingencies already planned.

How much do you cost?

Every package is priced on this page: $1,799 for the reception, $2,299 with ceremony and cocktail hour, $2,999 with uplighting and your monogram. Travel beyond 40 miles is quoted as a flat fee up front. Send your date and venue and we will confirm your exact total within one business day.

Is your date still open?

Peak Saturdays in wine country book early. Send us the date and we will tell you straight away, then walk you through what the night could look like.
