How booking works

Four steps, no mystery, no hard sell.

You will know the price, the plan and the person showing up long before the day arrives.

Start with your date
  • 1. Check the date. Send it over. We reply within one business day with a yes and a real quote.
  • 2. Lock it in. Simple contract, deposit, and the date is yours.
  • 3. Build the night. A planning call plus a shared timeline and song list you can edit until the week of.
  • 4. Show up and enjoy it. We load in early, sound check quietly, and handle the room from there.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

How far in advance should we book?

Popular Saturdays in wine country and the fall corporate season get booked six to twelve months out. That said, we typically keep room for short-notice events so if your date is next month, still ask us.

Do you travel to Tahoe, Reno, Monterey and wine country?

Constantly. Travel inside the greater Bay Area is included; for Tahoe, Reno, Monterey and the far reaches of wine country we add a flat travel fee that is quoted up front, never as a surprise line item.

Are you insured, and can our venue get a certificate?

Yes and yes. We carry general liability and send a certificate of insurance naming your venue as additional insured, usually within a week of signing.

Can we pick the music?

Absolutely. Every client gets a shared song list with must-plays, maybes and a do-not-play list that we take seriously. Then we read the room and keep your guests engaged.
