FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Are your playlists really school-appropriate?

Yes. We use clean radio edits only. You can have the full list ahead of time and can strike anything, no questions asked.

Can you take requests from students without losing control of the night?

We take requests through a single point, usually a notebook, and we filter every one against the approved list as well as our knowledge of specific artists. Students feel heard, adults stay comfortable.

Do you provide sound for a gym, a field or a cafeteria?

All three, and they need different rigs. Tell us the space and expected head count and we will bring gear scaled for it, including our own power distribution for outdoor events.

What does a school event usually cost?

Rates are published on this page: $550 for a rally, $850 for a dance, $1,200 for an auction or gala. Book four or more events in one school year and every one drops 15%. All-in pricing — no setup fees, no teardown fees.
