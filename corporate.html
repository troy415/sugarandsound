Run of show

How a corporate night actually flows.

Arrival & reception

Doors open to music already going. Warm, current, low enough to talk over. Nobody walks into an empty-sounding room.

Program & speeches

Mics tested and staged, walk-on music cued, timing kept tight. Your CEO gets applause, not feedback.

Dinner & mingling

Volume drops, groove stays. This is where we quietly test what this specific crowd reacts to.

Peak hour

Lights down, floor open, and the set built from what we learned during dinner. This is the part they post.

Last call & load-out

A clean close on schedule, then a quiet, fast strike so the venue loves you back.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Can you work with our AV team or venue’s in-house production?

Yes, and we prefer to. We will get on the call, split the channel list, and agree who owns which mic before load-in day.

Do you offer custom-branded music or playlists?

We build a brand-appropriate library for you in advance, cleared of anything off-message, and we will honor a do-not-play list with no fuss.

What about billing, POs and net terms?

We invoice through standard PO and net-30 processes and can be onboarded as a vendor before the deposit. Send us the portal link and we will fill out the forms.

Can you handle a multi-room or multi-day program?

Regularly, for example a general session in the morning, breakouts in the afternoon, after-party at night. We staff each room with its own operator and one lead who owns the whole schedule.

What does it cost?

Published on this page: $1,100 for a three-hour mixer, $1,700 for a four-hour company party, and custom scoping from $2,500 for multi-day or multi-room programs. Every quote includes the $2M certificate of insurance and the load-in plan your venue will ask for.
