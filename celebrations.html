FAQ

Questions we get a lot

How small is too small?

There is no floor. We play 30-person backyards and 400-person ballrooms with the same setup discipline... the gear just gets right-sized.

Our party is outdoors with no power nearby. Now what?

We bring battery systems and a generator plan. Tell us how far from an outlet you are and how big the lawn is, and we will spec it.

Can you keep it clean for the kids and still fun for the adults?

That is most of our private work. Clean edits through dinner and early evening, and we can open it up later once the little ones head home...your call.

Do you do multi-cultural or bilingual events?

Regularly. Latin, Filipino, Indian, Persian, and Chinese celebrations are all part of our normal calendar. Send us your family’s must-play traditions and we will come prepared, not improvising.

Got a milestone coming up?

Tell us who it is for and what they love. We will build a night around them and quote it in one email.
