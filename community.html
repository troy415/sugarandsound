Public space, public rules

We have done this with event organizers time and time again.

Outdoor community events come with sound ordinances, shared power, foot traffic and a neighbor who will absolutely call the city. Let's work together to plan for all four before we plug anything in.

Logistics

The unglamorous things that make an outdoor event work.

Send us the site map and conditions that may need special attention. We will handle it from there.

Talk to us about your event
  • Battery and generator power for sites with no reliable outlet.
  • Decibel-aware setup that respects local sound ordinances and keeps your permit clean.
  • Weighted, taped and coned speaker stands and cable runs in public foot traffic.
  • Clean, all-ages music by default at any daytime public event.
  • Certificate of insurance naming the city, park district or business association.
  • Rain and wind plan, including a covered position for the gear and a shortened strike.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Do you work with city permits and sound ordinances?

Constantly. Tell us the decibel limit and the cutoff time in your permit and we will build the setup around it, then keep a meter on hand so there is no worries later.

Can you provide your own power?

Yes. Battery-powered systems for smaller footprints and quiet generator setups for full-day events, so a missing outlet is never a problem.

Do you offer nonprofit or city rates?

The rates on this page are our public rates. For recurring markets and multi-date community series we price the season rather than the day — send us the full calendar and we will quote it as one line item your grant or PO can cover.

Can you MC the program too?

Yes, including vendor shout-outs, sponsor thank-yous, contest announcements, ribbon cuttings and handing the mic to elected officials without a single awkward pause.

Planning something for the whole neighborhood?

Send the site, the date and the permit conditions. We will come back with a plan, a quote and a COI for the city.
