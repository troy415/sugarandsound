Put your mix files in this folder.

1. Export as MP3 (128–192 kbps is plenty for streaming) or M4A.
2. Name the file in lowercase with dashes:  golden-hour.mp3
3. Open assets/data/mixes.js and add a block pointing at it.
4. Redeploy. The player, filters and deep links update on their own.

Keep individual files under about 90 MB. If a mix is longer than ~90 minutes,
export at 128 kbps mono-compatible stereo or split it into two parts.
